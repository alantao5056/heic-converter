Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 04/29/2025

This Privacy Policy explains how the HEIC to JPG Batch Converter ("the App") handles user data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how the App operates.

1. Data Access and Usage

The App only accesses image files located in the folder you explicitly select as the source folder. It does not access any other files or folders on your system unless you specifically choose them through the app interface.

2. Local Processing

All photo conversion operations are performed entirely on your local device. The App does not transmit, upload, or send any files, metadata, or personal information to any server, third party, or remote service.

3. No Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. This includes (but is not limited to) personal information, image contents, file names, or usage statistics.

4. No Internet Communication

The App does not require an internet connection and does not communicate with any online service during its operation.

5. Data Retention

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no data retention. All files and converted images remain exclusively on your local device under your control.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not use or integrate with any third-party services or SDKs that collect or share user data.

7. User Control

You maintain full control over your data. The App will only access folders you select and does not modify or delete files outside the scope of the conversion process.

8. Changes to This Policy

If any changes are made to this Privacy Policy in the future, we will update this document accordingly and indicate the effective date at the top.
